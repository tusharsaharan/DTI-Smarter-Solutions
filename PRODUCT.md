# Attune

## Register

product

## Users

Android users, especially college students, checking their phone between lectures,
deadlines and everyday activities. They need control over interruptions without
maintaining another complicated productivity system.

## Product Purpose

An installable on-device prototype of the five ideas documented in AGENTS.md and
PLAN.md. Real Android signals and actions follow Sense → Understand → Adapt →
Protect → Care. Smart Focus learns from a reviewed timetable and user decisions,
then offers a cancellable 60-second warning before applying real DND.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, trustworthy. Preserve the existing premium native app vocabulary and
direct, approachable copy rather than redesigning the product for each feature.

## Anti-references

No websites, 3D decoration, invented sensor readings, mock results, or hidden
automatic system changes. Avoid cluttered settings, inaccessible controls and
claims that a prediction is a measured fact.

## Design Principles

- System truth wins over optimistic interface state.
- Make automatic decisions visible, cancellable and reversible.
- Sensitive inputs and personal learning stay on the phone.
- Missing permissions and uncertain evidence have honest fallbacks.
- Build within the five existing capabilities and shared design tokens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain light and dark themes, labelled controls, readable text and existing motion
and haptic patterns. Countdown state must be understandable without colour or
animation and cancellation must remain easy to reach.
